About

I am a researcher at the Instituto de Ciencias Físicas of the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM). My work combines analytical theory and numerical calculations to understand how quantum systems interact with electromagnetic and magnetic environments.

Research

My research is organized around three closely connected areas.

01

Quantum light–matter interactions

Quantum emitters interacting with photons in cavities, waveguides, and superconducting circuits.

02

Quantum magnonics

Quantum spin waves and magnetic textures coupled to photons, circuits, and localized spins.

03

Structured environments

Engineered reservoirs producing long-range interactions, localized states, and non-Markovian dynamics.

Selected work

Recent themes include giant quantum emitters, ultrastrong waveguide QED, magnetic vortex quantum cavities, and quantum dynamics in structured photonic environments.
